Nostr WoT gives you **three themes and an automatic mode**. Pick the appearance that works for you in **Settings → Appearance and language**.

- **Light:** a bright background with purple accents.
- **Dark:** near-black surfaces, lighter hover states and purple accents.
- **La Crypta:** warm dark surfaces, cream text and lime-green accents inspired by Obelisk and [La Crypta](https://lacrypta.ar/).
- **System:** follows your device’s light or dark appearance automatically.

Your choice saves immediately and applies across the extension’s windows. QR codes keep a white background in every theme. Changing appearance leaves your identity and wallet settings in place.

The illustration compares the palettes; it is not a screenshot of the settings screen.

## Thank you, La Crypta

Thank you to **[La Crypta](https://lacrypta.ar/)** for giving us the space to present Nostr WoT in your weekly community call. We appreciate the opportunity to share the project with your community.

The La Crypta theme brings those colors into the extension. Try it from the appearance settings, or choose Light, Dark or System whenever you prefer.

These options are included in [Nostr WoT 0.8.3](https://github.com/nostr-wot/nostr-wot-extension/releases/tag/v0.8.3), available as a GitHub download. Browser-store publication is separate.
