## Make the extension feel like yours

Use a bright palette during the day or a darker one when you prefer a quieter background. System mode follows your device, so you do not have to change the appearance manually each time. Open **Settings → Appearance and language** to choose your look.

- **Light:** a bright background with purple accents.
- **Dark:** near-black surfaces, lighter hover states and purple accents.
- **La Crypta:** warm dark surfaces, cream text and lime-green accents.
- **System:** follows your device’s light or dark appearance automatically.

Your choice saves immediately and applies across the extension’s windows. QR codes keep a white background in every theme. Changing appearance leaves your identity and wallet settings in place.

## Create a theme

Want a different palette? Follow the [guide to creating extension themes](/guides/create-extension-theme) to apply custom colors or contribute a theme with light and dark modes. The guide covers color tokens, readable text, focus states and testing across the extension.

We created the La Crypta theme after [La Crypta](https://lacrypta.ar/) asked us for a dark theme. It is a small example of the collaboration we want to encourage between communities.
