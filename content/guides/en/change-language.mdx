Change the language of the Nostr WoT extension from **Settings → Appearance and language → Language**. Choose a language, then select **Confirm**.

## 1. Open Settings

Open the extension from your browser toolbar. Unlock it if needed, then choose **Settings** using the gear icon. Open **Appearance and language**.

![Settings with Appearance and language near the bottom](/images/guides/change-language/01-settings.png)

## 2. Open the language picker

Choose **Language** below the theme controls. The row shows your current language.

![The Language row below the appearance controls](/images/guides/change-language/02-appearance.png)

## 3. Choose and confirm

Scroll or drag the wheel until your language is centered. You can also focus the list and use the up and down arrow keys. The heading previews your choice before you apply it.

The available languages are **English, Español, Português, Deutsch, Français and Italiano**.

![Español selected in the language wheel, with Confirm below](/images/guides/change-language/03-spanish-selected.png)

Select **Confirm**. That button uses your current interface language until you confirm; in this English-to-Spanish example it still says “Confirm.” Closing the picker without confirming keeps the previous language.

## 4. Check the result

The language row now shows **Español**, and translated interface labels update. Your choice is saved for the next time you open the extension. Some appearance labels currently remain in English.

![The saved language is Español and the language label is now Idioma](/images/guides/change-language/04-spanish-applied.png)

## Switch back whenever you like

Return to the same section, open **Idioma**, select **English**, then choose **Confirmar**. Use the gear icon to find Settings if you do not recognize the current menu language.

Changing the interface language does not translate Nostr posts or messages, or change your accounts, keys or permissions. The website's language selector is separate from the extension's setting.

The screenshots show an English-to-Spanish change in a demo installation.
